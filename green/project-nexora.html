Work/NEXORA

NEXORA

A bold brand identity system built for a technology startup — fusing futuristic 3D form with purposeful visual language.

Category
Branding · 3D Art
Year
2024
Tools
Blender · Illustrator

Overview

The Brief

NEXORA is a tech startup brand identity project — a self-initiated concept exploring how a forward-looking company might present itself visually. The challenge was to create something that felt both premium and approachable, with a system that could scale across digital and physical touchpoints.

The visual direction drew from crystalline geometry, gradient light and architectural precision. Every element of the identity was designed to communicate intelligence, clarity and ambition.

Process

How it Came Together

01
Research & Direction
Studied brands in the tech space to identify white space — an opportunity for a cleaner, more architectural visual language.
02
3D Form Exploration
Built geometric 3D shapes in Blender to find the core visual symbol — a hexagonal form representing connection and structure.
03
Identity System
Developed the full system — logotype, colour palette, typography hierarchy and usage guidelines across digital touchpoints.

Deliverables

What Was Made

Logo & Symbol System
Primary mark, icon variants and monogram across light and dark usage.
Colour Palette & Typography
Defined brand colours and type hierarchy using Space Grotesk and Bebas Neue.
Digital Mockups
Applied to app UI, business cards and social media templates.
3D Brand Renders
Hero 3D renders for use in presentations and marketing materials.
