Work/Poster Design

POSTER
DESIGN

A series of experimental posters exploring the intersections of bold typography, geometric form and layered visual narrative.

Category
Graphic Design
Year
2024
Tools
Illustrator · Photoshop

Overview

The Series

This is a collection of self-initiated poster experiments built as a personal laboratory for pushing visual language. Each piece starts from a single constraint — a typeface, a colour rule, a shape — and explores how far that constraint can be taken before it breaks.

The work is deliberately diverse in mood and technique: some pieces are austere and typographic, others are layered and maximalist. Together they form a record of how my design thinking has evolved — and where it's going.

Process

How Each Piece is Made

01
Constraint Setting
Each poster begins with a single rule or restriction — a limited palette, one typeface, or a geometric starting point — that the composition must work within.
02
Sketch & Explore
Quick thumbnail sketches to lock composition before touching any software. At least 8–12 thumbnails per piece, then the most interesting two are taken further.
03
Refine & Finish
Executed in Illustrator for vector elements and Photoshop for texture and grain. Every piece goes through at least three distinct revision rounds.
