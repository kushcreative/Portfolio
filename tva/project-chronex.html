WORK / CASE 002

CHRONEX

A photorealistic 3D product render of a luxury timepiece — exploring material realism, lighting and cinematic composition.

Category3D Art · Product
Year2024
ToolsBlender · Photoshop

OVERVIEW

THE BRIEF

CHRONEX is a self-initiated 3D product visualization project — a study in photorealism applied to luxury product design. The goal was to produce a render that could sit alongside professional advertising imagery, with attention paid to every surface material, reflection and shadow.

The watch was built entirely in Blender, from the case geometry to the dial details, bracelet links and hands. The final render captures the weight and precision you'd expect from a real timepiece.

PROCESS

HOW IT CAME TOGETHER

01

Reference & Planning

Collected reference images of luxury watches to understand proportion, surface material and the visual vocabulary of the category.

02

3D Modelling

Built the watch in Blender — case, crown, bracelet links, dial details, hands and crystal — aiming for millimetre-level accuracy.

03

Lighting & Render

Set up a three-point product lighting rig using HDRIs and area lights, then refined materials using PBR shaders for maximum realism.
