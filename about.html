My Story

Design as a Language

I'm Kushal — a graphic designer, 3D artist and motion graphics creator who believes that design is never decoration. It's communication. Every shape, colour and transition should earn its place by saying something the words can't.

My work lives at the crossroads of technical precision and visual intuition. I came to design through a love of architecture and geometry, which is why so much of what I make is rooted in structure — even when the surface looks organic or abstract.

When I'm not pushing pixels, I'm studying material and light in Blender, pulling apart brand identities that fascinate me, or going down rabbit holes about typography and its history.
